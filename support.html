Use it

  1. Open Deckle.
  2. Paste text, drop a screenshot, capture a region of the screen, or paste a link.
  3. Press Read. Pause, the timeline, Previous, and Next move through the text.
  4. Save audio if you want a file. The voice is a Mac voice.

The $19 purchase

Deckle is a $19 one-time purchase. Buy $19 is in the window from the first open. The direct edition also starts a 14-day trial on that Mac. The window shows the days left. When the trial ends, Read, screenshot and region reading, link fetch, and Save audio stop until a license key is entered. Pressing Buy does not mark the app as purchased. The direct app has no card form. Buy opens the checkout address when one is set in commerce.json. The App Store edition uses Restore Purchases and stays locked until Apple reports the purchase. No App Store Connect record exists yet.

Common questions

Screen Recording. The first region capture may ask for permission. Recognition still happens on this Mac. If you deny it, capture stops. Pasted text and dropped images still work.

A link needs the network. Deckle cannot download a page while offline. After the words are here, listening and saving audio do not use the network.

Browser check. Deckle opens a window only when the response is a real browser-check interstitial. You finish that check yourself. It does not open that window for an ordinary page.

The trial ended. Speech is locked. Buy $19, then enter the license key in the field at the top of the window. A wrong key does not unlock the app. The window does not say the copy is purchased until that key checks out.
