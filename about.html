Privacy Terms Support

Deckle speaks with the voices installed on this Mac. Screenshot and screen-region text are recognized here. A link is downloaded from the site you name, then read here. There is no account.

Deckle is a $19 one-time purchase. The direct edition includes a 14-day trial on this Mac. Buy $19 stays in the window. When the trial ends, speech locks until a license key is entered. Pressing Buy does not mark the app as purchased. The App Store edition charges through Apple and stays locked until that purchase or a restore. No App Store Connect record exists yet.
