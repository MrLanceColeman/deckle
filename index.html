What you get

Price

The product is a $19 one-time purchase. The direct edition includes a 14-day trial, then speech locks until a license key is entered. Buy $19 is in the window and does not mark the app as purchased. macOS Speak Selection, Outloud, and SpeechDock remain free for text you can already highlight.

Fetching a link needs the network. Speech and screenshots do not.
